About

London is the capital city of one the oldest and most influential counties in the world, England later the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northen Ireland.

The city is 1,579 square kilometers and has a population of 9 million, so it is a very large city with many things to see and do from walking though the famous Trafalgar Square and seeing Nelson’s column standing triumphant in the middle of the square to working in London’s extensive business and tech sector in the inner city where new and old Londoners navigate one of the busiest cities in the world.
