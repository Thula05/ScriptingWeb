"The inhabitant of London could order by telephone, sipping his morning tea in bed, the various products of the whole earth... and reasonably expect their early delivery upon his doorstep."
— John Maynard Keynes

London is the financial engine of the United Kingdom with the city generating about 30 per cent of the UK's GDP. Its financial sector is world class and because of that many people work in offices. Many major companies like BP and Vodafone Group have their headquarters in London.

The tall almost omnipresent skyscapes are a sign of the city's wealth with millions of people working there. If you think the glass planes and gray concrete is boring just wait for night to see the gleaming light emanate from the financial capital of the world.
